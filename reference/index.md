# Package index

## Estimating prevalence

How many samples for a target margin of error, and the estimate with its
confidence interval.

- [`design_precision()`](https://mrc-ide.github.io/MMSpower/reference/design_precision.md)
  : Calculate sample size for a target margin of error when estimating
  prevalence
- [`estimate_prevalence()`](https://mrc-ide.github.io/MMSpower/reference/estimate_prevalence.md)
  : Estimate prevalence and its precision

## Testing against a threshold

Is prevalence above (or below) a decision threshold, such as the WHO
pfhrp2/3 deletion threshold?

- [`design_threshold()`](https://mrc-ide.github.io/MMSpower/reference/design_threshold.md)
  : Calculate sample size to detect a prevalence above or below a
  threshold
- [`test_threshold()`](https://mrc-ide.github.io/MMSpower/reference/test_threshold.md)
  : Test whether prevalence is above or below a decision threshold

## Detecting presence

How many samples to find at least one case of a rare variant, such as an
early drug-resistance marker?

- [`design_detection()`](https://mrc-ide.github.io/MMSpower/reference/design_detection.md)
  : Calculate sample size to detect at least one case

## Comparing two groups

Does prevalence differ between two groups, such as two regions?

- [`design_difference()`](https://mrc-ide.github.io/MMSpower/reference/design_difference.md)
  : Calculate sample size to detect a difference in prevalence between
  two groups
- [`test_difference()`](https://mrc-ide.github.io/MMSpower/reference/test_difference.md)
  : Test whether prevalence differs between two groups

## Trends over time

Is prevalence rising or falling across survey rounds?

- [`design_trend()`](https://mrc-ide.github.io/MMSpower/reference/design_trend.md)
  : Calculate sample size per timepoint to detect a linear trend in
  prevalence
- [`test_trend()`](https://mrc-ide.github.io/MMSpower/reference/test_trend.md)
  : Test whether prevalence is changing linearly over time

## Costing a study

- [`estimate_cost()`](https://mrc-ide.github.io/MMSpower/reference/estimate_cost.md)
  : Estimate total study cost
